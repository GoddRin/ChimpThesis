# Field verification of the satellite shorelines (printable)

*Purpose.* The shorelines in this study come from satellite imagery. Nobody has yet checked them on the ground. These ~20 points (file `outputs/field_validation/field_validation_points.csv`, also a `.gpkg` you can open in QGIS or load on a phone GPS app) are where a short visit gives the most information. **Nothing has been validated yet — do not write that it was until this form is filled in.**

**Equipment:** handheld GPS or phone with a GNSS app (note the accuracy shown), 30–50 m tape, camera, notebook/this form, the tide table for the day.

**For each point `Vnn`** (go to the listed longitude/latitude; if the beach is not accessible, record why):

| Item | What to record |
|---|---|
| Date, time, recorder | |
| GPS device and horizontal accuracy (m) | |
| Tide state / time of high and low water (from the tide table) | A waterline moves tens of metres with the tide; note it |
| Distance from the GPS point to the **vegetation line** (m, + seaward) | More stable than the waterline; the best field comparison for an erosion rate |
| Distance from the GPS point to the **waterline at the time of survey** (m) | |
| Evidence of erosion | scarp / undercut trees / exposed roots / collapsed structure / none |
| Protection structure present | none / seawall / revetment / riprap / breakwater / mangrove / other — and its condition, length, year built (ask residents or the MDRRMO) |
| Photos | file names; one looking along the shore each way, one at the structure |
| Notes | e.g. residents' account of how far the shore moved and since when |

**How the result will be used.** If the vegetation-line distances at the Medium points (Bulala Sur/Norte, parts of Dodan, Maura, Linao) are consistently seaward of the Low points, that supports the classification; large differences go into the limitations section. Residents' accounts ("the shoreline was about here in 1990") are weak evidence but valuable: record the place and take a GPS fix.
