Coastal erosion along eight Aparri barangays, 1990–2025

Academic use. Classification is based on the shoreline change rate only; it is not a full exposure/vulnerability assessment. Rates come from 30 m Landsat-based shorelines, with no measured positional error or image dates supplied (see “Limits”).

How to read this map

Each coloured line piece is 50 m of the open sea-facing shoreline. Its colour is the erosion rate from 1990 to 2025: Low (< 2 m/yr) · Medium (2–5) · High (> 5). Blue triangles mark pieces that grew seaward. Grey = not classified (transect fans at the river mouth). A dotted line = low confidence.

Click any piece for its numbers. Use the slider or ▶ to see how the shoreline moved; the 1990 line is the reference.
